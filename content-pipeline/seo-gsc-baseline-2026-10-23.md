## Reading
- Exact-match titles ("Split Airport to X Transfer") are already in place; routes rank 30 to 60 because the domain is new
  and has few links, not because of on-page text. Backlinks and age are the lever.
- Croatian pages convert best (24 of about 52 clicks); Croatian and Norwegian pages click at positions 5 to 7.
- English route queries also show the DE, SV, NO, HR and IT versions at positions 60 to 85: harmless, but those impressions are wasted.
- Old "X-to-split-airport" URLs still show in the report (merged and 301-redirected on 8 Oct); they should drop out.
- "makarska to split airport" gets about 100 impressions across 6 pages with no page above position 10: a return-trip
  section was added to the Makarska transport guide on 23 Oct.
