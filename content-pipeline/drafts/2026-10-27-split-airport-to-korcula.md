Split Airport to Korčula: Three Ways to the Island

## Body

Korčula is one of the most popular islands in the south of Croatia, and many guests land in Split and carry on
from there. The island is not reached by road all the way, so the real question is which crossing to use. There are three
sensible answers, and the right one depends on your luggage, your timing and where on the island you are staying.

### Option 1: Catamaran from Split

A passenger catamaran runs from the Split ferry port to Korčula Town. Travel sites put the direct crossing at roughly two
and a half hours, and in summer a second service calls at Brač and Hvar on the way and takes about three hours and a quarter. Timetables
change with the season, so check the operator before you plan your day around a departure.

This is the easiest option if you have little luggage and arrive early. The boat docks just below the old town of Korčula, so
you step off in the middle of things. The weak point is the transfer in Split: the ferry port is about twenty-five minutes from
the airport by car, and a catamaran will not wait for a late flight.

### Option 2: Drive to Orebić and take the car ferry

The other route goes by road to the Pelješac peninsula. You drive to Orebić, and from there a car ferry crosses to Dominče on Korčula in about
twenty minutes, according to the operator's timetable. Aggregators put the whole journey from Split Airport at roughly three and a half to
four hours including the crossing. Since the Pelješac Bridge opened, the road stays in Croatia and avoids the border crossing at Neum. Our drivers use the bridge.

The car ferry lands at Dominče, a few kilometres from Korčula Town, not in the old town itself. If you are staying in the town, the last leg is a short drive from the ferry.
There is also a foot passenger boat between Orebić and Korčula Town, but a car cannot use it.

This is the better choice for larger groups, families and anyone with heavy bags, because the luggage stays in one vehicle
from the airport to the door. It is also the option that does not depend on a single daily sailing.

### Option 3: Vela Luka, on the west side

If your accommodation is in Vela Luka, the car ferry and the catamaran from Split both go there. Sources put the car ferry at about three hours and the catamaran at about
two. Vela Luka is on the west side of the island, a long way from Korčula Town, so check which side your accommodation is on before choosing.

### Which one to pick

- **Small bags, early arrival, staying in Korčula Town:** the catamaran.
- **Family, large luggage or a late flight:** road and car ferry via Orebić, with one vehicle from the airport to Dominče or on.
- **Staying in Vela Luka:** the sea route from Split.

Whichever you pick, leave a buffer. Ferries run to a timetable, and a private transfer from the airport to the
port is the simple way to meet it. If your flight is delayed we follow the flight number and the driver adjusts the pickup.

Plan the return the other way around: the catamaran or the car ferry first, then the road. Allow a generous margin on the day of a flight, because the
ferry timetable is the part you cannot move. On busy summer days allow up to half an hour extra.

## FAQ (5)

**How do I get from Split Airport to Korčula?**
Either by car to the Split ferry port and the catamaran to Korčula Town, or by road to Orebić and the car ferry to Dominče. Check current timetables with the operators.

**How long does it take?**
Aggregators put the road and car ferry route at about three and a half to four hours from the airport, and the direct catamaran at about two and a half hours plus the transfer to the port. These are estimates; confirm before you travel.

**Can a private car go all the way to Korčula Town?**
A car can cross on the Orebić to Dominče ferry, which lands a few kilometres from the town. The foot passenger boat to the old town does not take vehicles.

**Is the Pelješac Bridge used on the way to Orebić?**
Yes. Our drivers take the bridge, so the road stays in Croatia.

**Should I book the ferry in advance?**
In July and August car ferry spaces and catamaran seats can sell out, so check the operator's site and book early in the season.
