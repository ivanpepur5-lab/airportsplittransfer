## Body

Split Airport is small, and that makes the first hour easy once you know the order of things. This guide follows a typical arrival from the plane door to the
car, so nothing about the first minutes in Croatia comes as a surprise.

### One terminal, one route

Split Airport has a single terminal. Arrivals and baggage claim are on the ground floor, departures on the floor above. There is nowhere to get lost:
you leave the aircraft, follow the signs to arrivals, and every passenger ends up in the same hall.

Where you come from decides one step. If your flight is from outside the Schengen area, you pass border control before you collect your bags. If you fly
from another Schengen country, you go straight to baggage claim. Your airline or ticket shows which group you belong to.

### Passport control and baggage

Allow extra time in July and August. Several flights often land within the same hour, and the queue at border control and the wait at the belt grow
with them. For visitors from outside the European Union, border checks now include a short registration of fingerprints and a photo on the first entry,
and later visits are faster. Check the official rules for your passport before you travel, because they have changed recently.

A realistic plan is to expect the arrivals hall some time after landing, not at the moment of landing. Do not book anything tight straight after it,
and tell your driver the flight number so the pickup follows your real landing time.

### Finding your driver

With a pre-booked transfer, the driver waits in the arrivals area, just beyond the exit from baggage claim. You will see a name board with the name you used when you booked.
If you cannot find the board, message us on WhatsApp and we guide you to the spot. The first 60 minutes of waiting are free, which covers a slow queue or a late bag.

We follow your flight number, so a delayed plane does not mean a missed pickup. If your plans change after you book, send us a message instead of waiting in the hall.

### Money and small things

Croatia uses the euro. An ATM is available in the arrivals area, and card payment is widely accepted, but keep a little cash for small purchases. If you need
a local SIM card or a coffee before the drive, there is time while the driver loads the bags, so just tell them.

### Other ways to reach the city

If you do not have a transfer, there are three options: the airport shuttle bus, a taxi from the rank outside arrivals and local bus routes along the coast.
Our guide to the three ways from the airport to Split compares them with the private transfer, so we do not repeat it here. One point is worth stating: bus service
is thinner late in the evening, so do not assume a bus will be waiting after a late landing.

Car rental desks are also in the arrivals area, for guests who prefer to drive themselves.

### A typical arrival, step by step

1. The plane parks and you leave the aircraft, following the signs to arrivals.
2. If your flight is from outside the Schengen area, you queue at border control.
3. You collect your bags at the belt on the ground floor.
4. You walk through the exit into the arrivals area, where your driver is waiting with the name board.
5. The driver helps with the luggage and walks you to the car.

Most of the waiting happens in steps 2 and 3, and those are the two steps nobody controls. That is why the pickup is planned around your real landing time and not around a fixed minute.

### Families and groups

Arriving with children or several suitcases changes the plan. Tell us the number of passengers and bags when you book, so the vehicle matches the group: a sedan for a small party, a Mercedes V-Class for up to 7 passengers or a Renault Trafic for up to 8. A child seat is free on request and is waiting in the car when you arrive, which is far easier than looking for one on the day.

### Landing late at night or very early

Night and early morning flights are the ones where a fixed pickup matters most. A driver who knows your flight number is waiting when you come out, and there is
no search for a taxi at an hour when the rank is quiet. For a morning departure, read our early-morning guide before you book.

### Before you fly

- Keep your booking confirmation and the WhatsApp number on your phone, with the phone charged.
- Write down your hotel or apartment address exactly as it appears on the booking.
- Have your flight number ready, and your passenger and luggage count.
- Remember the child seat. It is free and has to be requested when you book.

### Booking the transfer

Use the booking form on our homepage with Split Airport as the pickup. The price is fixed and quoted before you confirm. Enter your flight details and we do the rest.

## FAQ (5)

**How long does it take to leave Split Airport after landing?**
It depends on the time of day and whether border control applies to your flight. In summer, with several flights landing together, allow more time than usual.
Tell your driver the flight number so the pickup matches your real arrival.

**Where does my driver wait at Split Airport?**
In the arrivals area, just beyond the exit from baggage claim, holding a name board. If you cannot find the board, send us a message on WhatsApp.

**Is there an ATM and can I pay by card?**
There is an ATM in the arrivals area, and cards are widely accepted. Croatia uses the euro. Keep a little cash for small purchases.

**What if my flight to Split is delayed?**
We follow your flight number, so the driver adjusts the pickup. The first 60 minutes of waiting are free.

**Is the airport far from Split?**
No. The drive to the city centre takes around 25 to 30 minutes without traffic, longer on summer afternoons.
