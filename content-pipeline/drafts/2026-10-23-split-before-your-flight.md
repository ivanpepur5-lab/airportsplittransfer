### Timing: from check-out to the plane

Work backwards from your flight.

- Most airlines ask you to be at the airport about two hours before an international flight.
  Your ticket or app shows the exact time.
- The drive from the centre of Split to Split Airport is 22.8 km and takes about 28 minutes
  without traffic. Add time in July and August.
- So a flight at 20:00 means leaving the centre around 17:00, with a small buffer on top.

A sample day: check out at 10:00, drop bags at the storage by 10:30, lunch and a walk along the
Riva, pick up the bags at 16:30, driver at 17:00. Nothing to carry, nothing to rush.

### Booking your ride

Book the transfer from the storage to the airport (or to your next stop) in advance on the
booking form. Put the storage address as the pickup. Your driver waits there at the time
you choose.
