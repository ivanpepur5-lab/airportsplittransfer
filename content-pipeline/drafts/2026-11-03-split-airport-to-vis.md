## Body

Vis is the most remote of the central Dalmatian islands and one of the most rewarding. It has two ports, Vis Town on the north coast and
Komiža on the west, and it is the starting point for visits to the Blue Cave on the islet of Biševo. There is no bridge and no road link, so
getting there from Split Airport is a two-step journey: a drive to the Split ferry port, then a boat. This guide explains both.

### Step one: from the airport to the ferry port

Boats to Vis leave from the Split ferry port in the city centre. The drive from Split Airport takes around 25 minutes without traffic, the same
leg we describe on our Split page. In July and August allow more, because the port area is busy and the road from the airport is slow on summer afternoons.

Our drivers take you from the arrivals hall to the ferry port at a fixed price shown before you book. Tell us the departure time of your boat and
we plan the pickup to match. If your flight is late we follow the flight number, so a delayed landing does not mean a missed ferry.

### Step two: the crossing

There are two ways to reach Vis from Split, and the choice depends on whether you carry a car or only yourselves.

- **Catamaran (passengers only).** A fast boat runs from Split to Vis Town. Travel sites give the crossing as roughly one hour ten minutes to
  one hour twenty-five, depending on the operator. A year-round service runs daily. In summer an extra catamaran has been listed twice a day,
  and on one of the sailings it also calls at Bol on Brač and at Hvar Town, which makes the crossing longer.
- **Car ferry (cars and passengers).** The Jadrolinija car ferry takes about two hours and twenty minutes. It is the choice if you bring a vehicle.
  Services are less frequent than on the catamaran, so check the timetable before you plan the day around it.

Timetables change by season, and sources do not agree on the frequency in the summer months. Check the operator's website for the exact departures
on your date and book early for July and August.

### Which one to choose

- **Light luggage and no car:** the catamaran, the shortest crossing.
- **Own car or a lot of luggage:** the car ferry.
- **Visiting Hvar or Bol on the way:** the summer catamaran that calls there.

### Vis Town or Komiža?

Check which port your accommodation is near. Vis Town, in the north, is where
the Split boats arrive. Komiža, on the west coast, is the usual base for boat tours to the Blue Cave. If your hotel is in Komiža, plan a local transfer on the island after arriving.

### The Blue Cave

The Blue Cave is not on Vis itself but on the islet of Biševo, a short boat ride from Komiža. Tours are run by local operators, and the visit inside the cave is short.
We do not run these tours, so book them with the operator and check the season and the weather first.

### Going back to the airport

Plan the way back the other way round: the boat first, then the drive from the ferry port to Split Airport, about 25 minutes without traffic.
For a morning flight remember that the first catamaran may not leave early enough, so check the timetable before you book the flight. On busy summer days allow up to half an hour extra.

### Booking the transfer

Use the booking form on our homepage with Split Airport as pickup and the Split ferry port as the drop-off. The price is fixed before you confirm,
and the driver waits for your flight.

## FAQ (5)

**How do I get from Split Airport to Vis?**
Drive to the Split ferry port, then take the catamaran or the car ferry to Vis Town. The transfer from the airport to the port takes around 25 minutes.

**How long does the crossing take?**
The catamaran takes roughly one hour ten minutes to one hour twenty-five, the car ferry about two hours twenty minutes. Check the operator for current times.

**Can I bring a car to Vis?**
Yes, on the Jadrolinija car ferry. The catamaran carries passengers only.

**Where is the Blue Cave?**
On the islet of Biševo, a short boat ride from Komiža on the west coast of Vis. Tours are booked with local operators.

**Do you drive to Vis?**
We drive the transfer between Split Airport and the Split ferry port. On the island itself you arrange a local taxi or a rental. [IVAN: confirm, see question 2]
