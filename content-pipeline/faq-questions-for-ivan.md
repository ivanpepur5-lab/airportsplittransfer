# Questions for Ivan: real answers for the website FAQ (2026-10-14)

Why: Google's AI mode asks follow-up questions after someone searches for a
transfer. The pages should answer them with what Ivan really does, in his
words. Nothing here is answered yet and nothing may be invented: only answers
Ivan gives go on the site (EN first, then DE, SV, NO, HR, IT in one deploy).

Already answered on the homepage FAQ, so not asked again: price, destinations,
flight delay, return trip, vehicles, child seats, payment.

How to answer: a line or two per question is enough. "Don't offer that" is
also a valid answer. I will write the final wording and show it to Ivan before
anything goes live.

## Questions

1. **Meeting point.** Where exactly does the guest meet the driver at Split
   Airport (arrivals hall, outside, a fixed spot)? What does the guest see
   (name sign, where the driver stands)? What if the guest has no mobile data?
2. **Waiting.** The site says 60 minutes of free waiting. After a normal
   landing, how long do guests usually take to get out with their luggage?
   What happens if they need more than 60 minutes (do you wait, is it charged,
   how do you agree it)?
3. **Luggage.** How many suitcases fit comfortably in the Škoda Superb, the
   V-Class and the Trafic (large suitcases plus hand luggage)? What do you
   tell guests who travel with more?
4. **Pets.** Are small dogs or cats allowed? Anything guests must bring
   (carrier, blanket)?
5. **Stops on the way.** Can guests stop at a supermarket, ATM or pharmacy on
   the way to the hotel? For how long, and is it free or arranged?
6. **Leaving for the airport.** How early do you recommend guests be picked up
   for a flight (international, summer)? Do you have a rule of thumb for
   Split Airport, and does it change for Makarska, Dubrovnik or Zadar?
7. **Cancel and change.** The hero says "Free cancellation". Until when
   exactly (hours before pickup)? What happens if the guest is a no-show?
   Can they change the time or address by message?
8. **Language and driver.** Which languages do you speak with guests? Do
   guests get the driver's name and car before arrival (how, by WhatsApp or
   email)?
9. **Night and early flights.** Do you take bookings for very early or late
   flights (after midnight, before 5 am)? Anything guests should know?
10. **What guests ask most.** Which question do guests ask you again and
    again that is not on the site yet? (This one is the most valuable.)
