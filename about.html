About

A Private Transfer, Done Properly

Transfer Split Airport was built around a simple idea: the first and last hour of a trip should feel as good as the rest of it.

We operate private, fixed-price transfers between Split Airport and destinations across the Dalmatian coast — from the old town of Split itself to Dubrovnik, three and a half hours south. Every transfer is a single private vehicle for your party, not a shared shuttle.

Our drivers are licensed professionals who know the coast road, the ferry timetables and the fastest way around Split's old town traffic. Every flight is tracked in real time, so a delay on your end never means a missed pickup on ours.

The fleet is maintained to a consistent standard across all three vehicle classes — the Škoda Superb, the Mercedes-Benz V-Class and the Renault Trafic — so the level of care is the same whether you are travelling alone or with a group of eight.
