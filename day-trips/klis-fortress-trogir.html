Overview

A Fortress and an Island Town, Close to Split

Klis Fortress stands on a rocky pass above Split, with wide views over the city, the coast and the islands, and many visitors know it as a filming location of Game of Thrones. From there the coast road runs through the Kaštela villages to Trogir, an old town on a small island with stone lanes, a cathedral and a waterfront promenade, and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is an easy half day that also suits guests with a long wait before a flight.

What's Included

  • Private vehicle for your group only, no shared shuttle
  • English-speaking driver for the full day
  • Hotel or accommodation pickup and drop-off in Split
  • All fuel, tolls and parking
  • Flexible time at each stop. Your driver waits
  • Bottled water for the journey

Not included: Klis Fortress entrance tickets and any entrance fees in Trogir, paid separately on the day.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the Klis and Trogir trip take?

About 5 to 7 hours, including time at the fortress and in Trogir. Start and finish times are flexible and arranged with your driver.

What is included in the price?

Your private vehicle, English-speaking driver, all fuel, tolls and parking, and hotel or accommodation pickup and drop-off in Split are included. Klis Fortress tickets and any entrance fees are paid separately on the day.

Can this trip start or end at Split Airport?

Yes. We can collect you at the airport or drop you there after Trogir, which makes good use of a long wait between flights. Please tell us your flight times when you book.

Can I add Salona?

Yes. The Roman ruins of Salona lie close to Klis and can be added on request. Tell us when you book so we can plan the time.
