Overview

Two Countries, One Easy Day

Mostar sits on the Neretva River in Bosnia and Herzegovina, and its Old Bridge, Stari Most, is the symbol of the town: a single stone arch, rebuilt in 2004 and part of a UNESCO World Heritage area. The old bazaar, the stone houses and the riverside terraces are made for slow walking. On the way back, the Kravice waterfalls on the Trebižat river make a cool stop in summer. With a private driver you decide how long to stay at each place, and the driver takes care of the road and the border queue.

What's Included

  • English-speaking driver for the full day
  • Hotel or accommodation pickup and drop-off in Split
  • All fuel, tolls and parking

  • Bottled water for the journey

Suggested Itinerary

A Typical Day to Mostar

  • 08:00

    Pickup in Split

    An early start beats the traffic and the heat. Your driver collects you from your hotel or apartment.

  • ~10:45

    Arrival in Mostar

    Your driver drops you near the Old Town and agrees a meeting time. Start with the bridge and the old bazaar while it is still quiet.

  • 10:45 – 14:00

    Old Town and Lunch

    Cross Stari Most, walk through the stone lanes of the bazaar and have lunch on a terrace above the river.

  • ~15:00

    On the drive back, stop at the Kravice waterfalls for a walk and, in summer, a swim if you would like to add it.

  • ~19:00

    Drop-off in Split

This is a suggested schedule: departure time, lunch stop and time spent in the park are all flexible and arranged directly with your driver.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the Mostar and Kravice day trip take?

Plan on 10 to 12 hours door to door, including time in Mostar and at the Kravice waterfalls. The drive each way takes roughly 3 hours, depending on traffic and the border.

Do I need a passport for the Mostar trip?

Yes. The trip crosses the border into Bosnia and Herzegovina, so everyone needs a valid passport or the ID card required for their nationality. Please check the entry rules for your nationality before you travel. Your driver takes care of the road and the border queue.

What is included in the price?

Your private vehicle, English-speaking driver, all fuel and tolls, and hotel or accommodation pickup and drop-off in Split are included. Entrance fees, such as the Kravice waterfalls, and food and drinks are paid separately on the day.

Can the day be changed?

Yes. Departure time, time in Mostar and the stop at the Kravice waterfalls are flexible and arranged with your driver. Tell us if you would like to add another stop.
