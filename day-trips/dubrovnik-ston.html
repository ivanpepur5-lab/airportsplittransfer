Overview

The Pearl of the Adriatic in One Long Day

Dubrovnik is the most visited city on the Croatian coast, with its walled Old Town, the Stradun and the sea views from the city walls. On the way, Ston is worth a stop: a small town on the Pelješac peninsula with some of the longest fortification walls in Europe, and the oyster beds of Mali Ston nearby. It is a long day, around 12 hours including the driving, but with a private driver you travel at your own pace, with no coach timetable and no changes.

What's Included

  • Private vehicle for your group only, no shared shuttle
  • English-speaking driver for the full day
  • Hotel or accommodation pickup and drop-off in Split
  • All fuel, tolls and parking
  • Flexible time at each stop. Your driver waits
  • Bottled water for the journey

Not included: tickets for the Dubrovnik city walls and the Ston walls, food, drinks and oyster tasting, all paid separately on the day.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Dubrovnik and Ston day trip?

Plan on around 12 to 13 hours door to door. The drive is about 3.5 hours each way, with time at Ston and in Dubrovnik.

What is included in the price?

Your private vehicle, English-speaking driver, all fuel, tolls and parking, and hotel or accommodation pickup and drop-off in Split are included. Tickets for the city walls and the Ston walls, food, drinks and oyster tasting are paid separately on the day.

Is one day enough for Dubrovnik?

One day gives you the Old Town, the city walls and a relaxed lunch. If you would like more time, we can also arrange a one-way transfer to Dubrovnik and a pickup on another day.

Can I skip Ston?

Yes. The stop at Ston is optional and the day can be shortened or changed. Tell us your plans when you book.
