Mercedes V-Class vs Renault Trafic: Choosing the Right Group Vehicle

For groups too large for a standard sedan, the choice usually comes down to two vehicles: the Mercedes-Benz V-Class and the Renault Trafic. Both are capable, well-maintained options — the difference is in scale and finish rather than reliability.

Capacity

The V-Class comfortably seats up to seven passengers with full luggage, making it the natural choice for larger families or small groups of friends. The Trafic extends that to eight, with more cargo space behind the last row — the better option once a group exceeds what the V-Class can carry without stacking bags on laps.

Cabin experience

The V-Class is the fleet's premium option: leather seating, a quieter cabin, and captain's-chair seating in the middle row for longer journeys such as Split to Dubrovnik or Plitvice. The Trafic is built more for practicality — a genuinely comfortable ride, but with a van-style bench-seat layout better suited to shorter transfers or larger groups where interior finish matters less than simply fitting everyone in with their bags.

Pricing

Because both vehicles are priced identically on every route in the fleet, the decision doesn't need to weigh cost against comfort — it comes down purely to group size and preference. A party of five with a business-trip feel might still choose the V-Class over the Trafic even though either could technically carry them.

Which to book

As a simple rule: choose the V-Class for parties of up to seven where cabin comfort matters, and the Trafic once the group reaches eight or luggage volume is unusually high — a wedding party with garment bags, for instance, or a group returning from a week-long stay with full suitcases.
