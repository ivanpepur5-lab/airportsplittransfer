Early Morning Transfer to Split Airport

Many flights out of Split leave between 6 and 8 in the morning, especially budget airlines and charter flights on changeover days. That means a check-in around 4–5am — and the hardest part of the trip is often getting to the airport before the day has started.

Why early mornings are tricky

  • Buses aren't running yet, or only a few are. Check the first airport shuttle departure against your flight time before you rely on it.
  • Ride-hailing apps are unreliable at 4am. There may be no cars nearby, or a driver may accept and then cancel.
  • Street taxis are rare outside the centre of Split, and almost non-existent in smaller towns along the coast.

How early should you leave?

Aim to be at the airport 2 hours before departure in summer. Split Airport's terminal is modern but busy in peak season, and security queues build quickly in the morning wave of departures. Then add the drive time:

  • Split city centre: 25–30 minutes
  • Trogir: 10–15 minutes
  • Podstrana: around 30 minutes
  • Omiš: around 45 minutes
  • Makarska: around 1 hour 20 minutes

Roads are quiet at that hour, so drive times are usually reliable. The risk is the time before the car arrives, not the drive.

Book the day before, not on the morning

A pre-booked transfer is the calm option. The driver is at your door at the agreed time, and the price is the same at 4am as at noon — there's no night rate. When you book, give us your flight time and we'll suggest a pickup time. If anything changes, you can move or cancel the booking free of charge.

Tip: if you book your arrival and departure together as a return trip, you get 5% off both ways and never have to think about the return at the end of your holiday.

Checklist for the night before

  • Double-check the pickup time and address in your booking confirmation.
  • Tell us if your accommodation has a hard-to-find entrance or gate.
  • Check your flight status in case of a time change.
  • Have your passport and boarding pass ready — you'll be sleepy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a bus to Split Airport early in the morning?

Service at very early hours is limited. Check the first airport shuttle departure against your flight time before you rely on it.

How early should I get to Split Airport?

About 2 hours before departure in summer, when morning security queues are longest.

Do you charge extra for early morning transfers?

No. The price is the same at any time of day, with no night rate.
