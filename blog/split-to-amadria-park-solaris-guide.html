Split Airport to Amadria Park Hotel Solaris Šibenik: Taxi Transfer Guide (2026)

Traveling from Split Airport (SPU) to Amadria Park Hotel Solaris in Šibenik? A private airport transfer is the fastest and most comfortable way to reach the resort, with door-to-door service and a fixed price.

How far is Amadria Park Solaris from Split Airport?

  • Distance: approximately 60 km (37 miles)
  • Travel time: 50–70 minutes
  • Route: A1 motorway and the Adriatic coastal approach to Šibenik

Most transfers take around one hour, depending on traffic and the season.

Meet & Greet at Split Airport

Your driver will meet you in the Arrivals Hall with a name sign after your flight lands. We monitor flight arrivals, so if your plane is delayed, your pickup time is adjusted automatically at no extra charge.

Why choose a private transfer?

  • Fixed price with no hidden fees
  • Direct transfer to your hotel
  • Flight tracking included
  • Professional English-speaking driver
  • Child seats available on request
  • Comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle

About Amadria Park Hotel Solaris

Amadria Park is one of Croatia's premier seaside resorts, located just south of Šibenik. The resort includes several hotels, family attractions, beaches, restaurants, and a marina, making it a popular destination for couples, families, and conference guests.

When booking your transfer, it's helpful to specify your exact hotel within the Amadria Park complex to ensure direct drop-off at the correct entrance.
