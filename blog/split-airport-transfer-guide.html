Split Airport Transfer Guide: Everything You Need to Know

Split Airport (SPU) sits on the coast around twenty minutes from the old town, at the base of the Kaštela bay. Despite the short distance, how you make that first leg of the trip sets the tone for the whole holiday — arrive tired and disoriented after a long queue, or step straight into a private car with your name on a board at arrivals.

Your options from the terminal

There are three practical ways to reach the city: the shuttle bus timed to flight arrivals, a rank taxi outside the terminal, or a pre-booked private transfer. The shuttle bus is the cheapest option but runs to its own schedule, drops passengers at the main bus station rather than a specific address, and can mean standing with luggage in July heat. Rank taxis are metered and available on demand, but pricing can vary and there's no guarantee of vehicle size for a group with multiple bags.

Why travellers choose a private transfer

A pre-booked private transfer removes the two biggest sources of arrival stress: uncertainty over price, and uncertainty over capacity. The fare is fixed and agreed before you fly, the vehicle is matched to your party size and luggage, and the driver is waiting at arrivals rather than found in a queue. For families, groups, or anyone arriving late at night, that certainty is worth more than the small premium over a shared shuttle.

What to have ready when you book

A smooth pickup depends on a few details: your flight number (so the driver can track delays), passenger count, luggage count, and the exact drop-off address. If you're connecting onward to the islands via ferry, mention your ferry time — the driver can time the drive to make the crossing comfortably rather than at a sprint.

Getting back to the airport

The same logic applies in reverse for departures. Build in extra time for Split's summer traffic around the old town and the airport road, particularly between June and September. A private transfer booked for departure removes the guesswork of finding a taxi during a busy morning departure bank.
