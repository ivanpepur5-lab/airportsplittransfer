Klis Fortress and Salona: A Half Day Between Split Airport and the City

Most guests land at Split Airport, drive straight to their hotel and then wait for check-in. If your flight arrives in the morning, there is a better use of those hours. Two of the best sights near Split lie almost on the way: Klis Fortress on the ridge above the city, and the Roman ruins of Salona in Solin.

Together they make an easy half day, and neither needs a full transfer route of its own. This guide explains what to see, how long to plan and how a private driver fits in.

Klis Fortress

Klis Fortress stands on a rocky ridge above the village of Klis, on the old pass between the mountains Mosor and Kozjak. For centuries this pass was the way inland from the coast, which is why so many rulers wanted the fortress. The Ottomans took it in 1537 after a long defence led by Petar Kružić, and the Venetians won it back in the 17th century.

Today you walk through gates and stone corridors up to a long terrace with a wide view over Split, the islands and the sea. Many visitors also know it as a Game of Thrones filming location, where it stood in for the city of Meereen.

What to know before you go:

  • The climb is not very demanding, but in summer the heat can make it tiring. Bring water and wear comfortable trainers.
  • Avoid the strongest sun. Go early or late in summer.
  • Plan on roughly an hour to an hour and a half at the fortress. Most guests are delighted by the view over Split and the islands.

Salona

Salona, today the town of Solin, was the capital of the Roman province of Dalmatia. The emperor Diocletian came from this region, and his palace in Split, built around the year 300, is the reason the city exists. Salona is where that story starts.

The site is open ground with old walls, the remains of an amphitheatre, early Christian churches and a cemetery area. It is quiet compared with Split's old town. Plan on 45 minutes to an hour, and bring water and a hat, because there is not much shade.

How the half day fits together

The order that works best for most guests is:

  1. Land at Split Airport and meet your driver in the arrivals hall.
  2. Drive to Klis first, while it is cooler.
  3. Continue to Salona, a short drive from Klis.
  4. Finish in Split, at your hotel or at Diocletian's Palace.

This keeps the driving short and the sightseeing in one direction, and your luggage stays with the driver the whole time. For both sights together, allow at least two to three hours, not counting the drive.

Klis and Salona compared

Klis is about views and atmosphere. You climb, you look out over Split and the sea, and you get a feel for why this pass mattered. Salona is about history on the ground. You walk among the remains of a Roman city that once stood next to the sea, and you can read the layout of streets, walls and public buildings. Guests usually find Klis more exciting because of the fortress and the view, while Salona suits those who are interested in history and archaeology. If you only have time for one, choose Klis for scenery and photos, and Salona if you want a quiet hour and an easy walk.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far are Klis Fortress and Salona from Split Airport?

Both are close to Split, on the same side of the city as the airport. Klis is around 20 minutes by car and Salona (Solin) around 15 minutes, depending on traffic. Klis to Salona is a short drive of about 10 to 15 minutes.

Can I visit both in one morning?

Yes. We recommend at least two to three hours for both sights together, not counting the drive. If your flight lands early in the day, you can do both and still reach Split by lunchtime.

Do I need to book tickets in advance?

Normally you can buy tickets at the entrance, but opening hours and prices change by season, so check the official pages shortly before you go. In peak summer it is sensible to start early.

Is Klis Fortress suitable for children or people with limited mobility?

Children usually enjoy the fortress. The climb is not very demanding, but the paths are uneven stone and summer heat makes it harder. If anyone in your group finds uneven ground hard, mention it when booking.

Can the driver wait while I visit?

Yes. The driver waits at the car park near the fortress entrance, or returns for you at an agreed time if you plan a longer visit. The price is agreed in advance and depends on how long you stay, so tell us when you book.
