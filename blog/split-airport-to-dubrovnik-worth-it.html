Split Airport to Dubrovnik: Is It Worth It?

Many travellers heading to Dubrovnik end up flying into Split instead: because flights are cheaper, there are more routes, or they want to see both cities. The question is whether the 230 km in between is a problem or part of the trip. (For the route itself and places to stop, see our Split to Dubrovnik route guide.)

When it makes sense

  • The flight price difference is big. For a family, saving €100 or more per ticket on flights into Split easily covers the drive.
  • You want to see Split too. Fly in, spend a few days in Split, then transfer to Dubrovnik and fly home from there.
  • There's no direct flight from your city to Dubrovnik on your dates, especially outside summer.

If you're flying in only to go straight to Dubrovnik for a short stay, and a direct flight to Dubrovnik costs about the same, flying direct is simpler.

How long does it take?

By car, 3 to 3.5 hours. The motorway covers the first part; the rest follows the coast. Since the Pelješac Bridge opened in 2022, the route can avoid the short stretch through Bosnia and Herzegovina at Neum. Keep passports handy anyway, drivers sometimes choose the Neum road depending on traffic.

What a transfer costs

A private transfer costs around €380 in a car for up to 4 and around €455 in a van for up to 8, per vehicle. For a group of eight that's about €57 per person: not far from the catamaran, but directly from the arrivals hall to your hotel door in Dubrovnik, with no connection to make.

It also turns the drive into part of the holiday. With a private driver you can stop for photos, coffee, or lunch in Ston for oysters. Tell us what you'd like when booking and we'll plan the time.

The verdict

For couples on a budget, the bus is fine. For families and groups with luggage, a private transfer is usually the most comfortable way: and when the flight savings are big, it often pays for itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the drive from Split Airport to Dubrovnik?

About 3 to 3.5 hours, depending on traffic and the route.

Do I need a passport to drive from Split to Dubrovnik?

The route over the Pelješac Bridge stays in Croatia, but keep passports with you in case the driver uses the road through Neum in Bosnia and Herzegovina.

How much is a transfer from Split Airport to Dubrovnik?

Around €380 for a car with up to 4 passengers and around €455 for a van with up to 8, per vehicle.
