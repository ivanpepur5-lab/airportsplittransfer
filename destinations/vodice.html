How much does a transfer from Split Airport to Vodice cost?

Can I book a return transfer?

Yes — select "Return" when booking to schedule both legs of your trip together. A return transfer automatically includes a 10% discount on the return leg.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight number in real time, so your driver adjusts the pickup automatically. There is no extra charge for reasonable delays.
