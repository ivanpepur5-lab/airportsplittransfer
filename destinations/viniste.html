Journey Overview

The Route to Vinišće

Vinišće is one of the shorter runs on this list — twenty kilometres and around twenty-five minutes, leaving the airport on the same road used for Trogir before turning off along the bay a little further north. The approach road hugs the inlet for its final stretch, giving a clear view of the anchored yachts before the village itself comes into sight, a pleasant, unhurried way to end a flight. Even at this short distance the scenery shifts noticeably — the busier hotel strip around Trogir gives way to a quieter run of olive terraces and small stone jetties as the road tracks the bay's edge, and most of the drive stays close enough to the water that the sea is rarely out of view. Because the whole journey takes under half an hour, it's a popular choice for visitors who'd rather spend their first hour in Croatia settling in at their accommodation than sitting in a car, and the fixed price means there's no temptation to rush the driver on what is already a short, easy run.

About Vinišće

What to Expect on the Ground

Vinišće's bay is one of the most sheltered on the Trogir riviera, which is exactly why sailors have used it as a safe anchorage for generations — you'll usually find a scattering of yachts riding at anchor even outside the main charter season. On land, the village is modest: a waterfront row of restaurants serving the boats as much as the holidaymakers, a small pebble beach that shelves gently enough to suit small children, and little else in the way of organised tourism infrastructure. That's the draw — Vinišće has stayed a genuine fishing-and-sailing village rather than turning into a resort, even as Trogir itself, a short drive away, has grown busier every year. The surrounding hillside is planted with olives and a scattering of vineyards, and a short coastal path connects the main bay to a couple of smaller, quieter coves worth the walk for anyone staying more than a night or two. The bay's depth and shelter have also made it a popular spot for small-scale mussel and oyster farming, visible as lines of buoys further out in the water, a genuinely local industry that's been part of the bay's economy for decades. Restaurants along the front tend to serve whatever the boats bring in that day rather than a fixed menu, which rewards visitors happy to ask what's fresh rather than order from habit.

What to Expect During the Transfer

The driver waits at arrivals with a name board and tracks your flight number automatically, so a delayed landing doesn't turn into a longer wait once you're through the terminal. It's a direct transfer with no other stops — your luggage goes in at the airport and comes out at your accommodation in Vinišće, with the driver on hand to help either end. Given the short distance, this is also a popular route for guests staying in Trogir who want a quieter day out at Vinišće's beach without the hassle of parking a rental car — the driver can wait or arrange a return pickup at an agreed time if you'd rather not book two separate one-way trips for a day visit. Couples and small groups travelling with just hand luggage often find the Sedan more than sufficient for this particular route, keeping the cost as low as possible for a genuinely short hop.

Popular Nearby

Places Worth Knowing About

Trogir's UNESCO-listed old town is a short drive away and easily combined with a Vinišće stay for a day trip. Seget Donji, right on Trogir's doorstep, and the marina town of Rogoznica further north are both within easy reach if you'd like to explore more of this stretch of coast during a longer visit. The small village of Drvenik, a short drive further along the coast, is the departure point for regular ferries to the island of Hvar, worth knowing about if an island day trip is part of your plans. Vinišće's own coastline also hides a couple of smaller, less-visited coves a short walk beyond the main bay, reachable along a coastal path that most visitors staying only a night or two never discover. For a longer stay, the island of Šolta is also reachable by ferry from the Split area, offering a genuinely quiet alternative day trip away from the more visited Hvar and Brač, with a handful of small konoba restaurants near its own harbour worth the crossing on their own merits.

Common Questions

How much is a private transfer from the airport to Vinišće?

€45 one-way in the Sedan (1–4 passengers) or €65 in the Business Van or Van (1–7–8 passengers), fixed regardless of exact drop-off address within the village.

Is Vinišće's beach suitable for young children?

Yes — the bay is sheltered and shelves gently, which is part of why it's long been popular with sailing families as well as holidaymakers with small children.

Can I book a return transfer for departure day too?

Yes, though note a return is priced as two one-way transfers with no discount applied, so the total is simply double the one-way fare.

How close is Vinišće to Trogir itself?

Trogir is a short onward drive from Vinišće, easily arranged as a separate point-to-point transfer if you want to explore the old town during your stay.

Do you take bookings for early morning or late night flights?

Yes, we run 24 hours a day, year-round — early and late flights carry no surcharge.
