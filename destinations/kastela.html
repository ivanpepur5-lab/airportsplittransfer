Journey Overview

The Route to Kaštela

Split Airport is not just close to Kaštela, it is part of it: the runway sits at the western end of the bay, beside Kaštel Štafilić. That makes this the shortest transfer on our list. The seven villages stretch roughly 17 km east along the shore towards Split, so the drive depends on which one you are staying in.

Kaštel Štafilić and Kaštel Novi are only a few minutes from arrivals. Kaštel Stari, Lukšić and Kambelovac sit in the middle of the bay, while Kaštel Gomilica and Kaštel Sućurac are at the eastern end, closest to Split. Even there, the drive usually takes around 15 to 20 minutes outside the summer rush. The main road runs above the villages, and your driver takes you to the exact address, down to the narrow lanes by the sea.

About Kaštela

Seven Villages, One Bay

Kaštela takes its name from the castles (kaštel) built along this shore in the 15th and 16th centuries. Noble families and church orders from Split and Trogir fortified their estates here as protection against Ottoman raids, and a village grew up around each one. Seven of them survive as the settlements you see today, from Kaštel Sućurac in the east to Kaštel Štafilić in the west.

Several of the castles are worth a short walk. Kaštilac in Kaštel Gomilica, a small fortress built by Benedictine nuns and joined to the shore by a stone bridge, appeared as Braavos in Game of Thrones. Vitturi Castle in Kaštel Lukšić houses the town museum and is tied to the local legend of Miljenko and Dobrila, often called Croatia's Romeo and Juliet. In Kaštel Štafilić, an olive tree estimated to be around 1,500 years old still grows near the sea.

Today Kaštela is a lived-in town rather than a resort. Most visitors stay in private apartments and holiday homes, with a few hotels along the bay. It makes a practical, quieter base between Split and Trogir, with small pebble beaches and long stretches of seafront promenade. Evenings are quiet, with konobas and cafés along the waterfront in most villages.

What to Expect During the Transfer

Your driver follows your flight number, so if you land early or late the pickup moves with you. You are met in the arrivals hall and walked to the car with help for your luggage. Because the drive is so short, Kaštela suits late-night arrivals especially well: you are at your door minutes after leaving the terminal. Apartment addresses here are often in narrow streets near the sea, so add the house number, your host's name or a short description in the booking notes; drivers who live and work in Kaštela know these lanes well. For the return to the airport, the short distance means little buffer is needed, even for the first flights of the morning.

Getting Around

Once You're in Kaštela

The villages are joined by the seafront promenade and by the main road above them, so it is easy to walk or cycle between neighbouring castles. Local buses run along the bay towards Split and Trogir. Many guests use Kaštela as a base, take a short private ride to Split's old town or to Trogir for the day, and come back to a quieter evening by the sea. In July and August the main road can be slow in the late afternoon, so allow extra time if you are heading into Split.

If you are travelling with a family, the calm water of the bay and the short distances between villages make Kaštela an easy, low-stress first stop. Child seats are free on request, so mention the age of each child when you book.

Popular Nearby

Places Worth Knowing About

Trogir, with its UNESCO-listed old town, lies just past the airport at the western end of the bay. In the other direction, Split and Diocletian's Palace are a short drive east, as is the ferry port for the islands. Along the Trogir Riviera, Seget Donji offers beaches and marinas a few minutes further on. For a full day out, Krka National Park is an easy private day trip from a Kaštela base. Which Kaštela villages do you cover?

All seven: Kaštel Štafilić, Novi, Stari, Lukšić, Kambelovac, Gomilica and Sućurac. Enter your exact address in the booking form and the driver will take you to the door.

Is a private transfer worth it for such a short distance?

For most guests, yes. You are met in arrivals, helped with your luggage and driven straight to an address that can be hard to find in the narrow lanes by the sea, at a fixed price agreed before you travel.

Can I combine a Kaštela transfer with a stop in Split or Trogir?

Yes, multi-stop requests are common here since Kaštela sits between Split and Trogir. Mention any additional stop when you book and we'll confirm it as part of the same fixed-price journey.
