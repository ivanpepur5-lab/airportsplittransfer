Your Private Transfer to Medora Auri Family Beach Resort

With a journey of well over an hour, a private transfer is the difference between arriving relaxed and arriving worn out. There's no shared shuttle making stops at other hotels along the way — you go straight from the terminal to the resort's entrance in Podgora, with room for the pram, the beach bags and everything else a family brings on holiday.

Transfer Time from Split Airport

Podgora is around 102 km south of Split Airport, and the drive normally takes about 1 hour 35 minutes down the coastal road through the Makarska Riviera. It's a scenic route along the water with Biokovo rising on one side, and your driver knows it well enough to keep the pace comfortable for children and tired travellers alike.

Why Choose a Private Transfer

For a drive this long, comfort matters — a private vehicle means no unplanned stops, no other families' luggage taking up space, and a fixed price for the whole car regardless of how many of you are travelling.

  • No shared shuttle — direct to the resort entrance

About Medora Auri Family Beach Resort

Medora Auri Family Beach Resort is built specifically around family holidays, with a private pebble beach, multiple pools including areas for children, and a layout designed for longer stays rather than short city breaks. Podgora itself is a quieter stretch of the Makarska Riviera than Makarska town a little further north, with the Biokovo Nature Park rising steeply behind it — a good match for families who want beach time and mountain scenery without the busier centres.

Since Podgora is well south along the coast, it's worth planning the rest of your trip around it too — check our other routes from Split Airport, or arrange a private day trip to Plitvice Lakes or Krka National Park before you head down to the resort.
