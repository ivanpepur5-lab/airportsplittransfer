Your Private Transfer to Hotel Le Meridien Lav

Skip the shared shuttle and the taxi queue outside arrivals — a private transfer takes you straight from Split Airport to the hotel's own driveway, with no other passengers, no detours and no extra stops along the way. Your driver already knows the resort's entrance and drop-off point, so check-in can start within minutes of leaving the terminal.

Transfer Time from Split Airport

Podstrana lies about 12 km south of the airport, and the drive to Hotel Le Meridien Lav normally takes around 15 minutes along the coastal road that skirts Split's western bay. It's one of the shortest airport transfers on this coast, which makes it easy to fit around any flight time, including late-night arrivals.

Why Choose a Private Transfer

About Hotel Le Meridien Lav

Hotel Le Meridien Lav is a large seafront resort in Podstrana with its own stretch of beach, several outdoor pools, a spa and wellness centre, and facilities that suit both a relaxed beach holiday and a conference or wedding stay. Its position right on Split's bay means guests get resort-style space and quiet without being far from the city — the old town and Diocletian's Palace are a short drive away, and the airport transfer itself is one of the quickest on the coast.

Staying at Hotel Le Meridien Lav also puts the rest of the coast within easy reach — browse our other routes from Split Airport or add a private day trip to Plitvice Lakes or Krka National Park while you're here.

Common Questions

How long does the transfer from Split Airport to Hotel Le Meridien Lav take?

The drive takes around 15 minutes under normal traffic conditions, since Podstrana is only about 12 km from the airport.

How much does a private transfer to Hotel Le Meridien Lav cost?

The exact price depends on your vehicle choice and is fixed before travel with no hidden fees — enter your details on our home page booking form for an instant quote.

Will the driver wait if my flight is delayed?

Yes. We monitor your flight number in real time and adjust the pickup automatically, at no extra cost for a reasonable delay.

Can I book a return transfer to the airport?

Yes — select "Return" when booking to arrange both legs together, which also applies an automatic 5% discount to the total price.

What vehicles are available for the transfer to Hotel Le Meridien Lav?

We offer a Sedan for up to 3 passengers and a Business Van/Van for larger groups or extra luggage, both at a fixed price per vehicle.
