Your Private Transfer to Hotel Ambasador Split

A private transfer takes you from the terminal directly to the hotel's own drop-off on Bačvice, with no shared shuttle stops and no taxi rank to negotiate. Your driver already knows the beachfront access road, so you're checked in and looking at the sea within half an hour of landing.

Transfer Time from Split Airport

Hotel Ambasador Split is around 21 km from the airport, and the drive typically takes about 27 minutes along the coast road that runs past central Split before reaching Bačvice. It's one of the more central hotel locations on this list, close enough to town that light traffic can occasionally add a few minutes, which is why we plan pickups with a buffer either way.

Why Choose a Private Transfer

With a private car, the fare is fixed for the vehicle rather than per head, so a couple, a family or a small group of friends all pay the same set price. You also get a driver working solely for your group from the moment you land.

  • No shared shuttle — direct to the hotel entrance
  • One fixed price per vehicle, not per passenger
  • Flexible pickup, any time of day or night
  • Spacious vehicles for families and groups

Meet & Greet at Arrivals

Your driver waits inside the arrivals hall with a name board, ready to help with bags and take you straight to the car — no wandering the car park looking for a pickup point. We track your flight number in real time, so early or delayed landings are already factored in at no extra charge.

Fixed, All-Inclusive Pricing

You're quoted a fixed price before travelling, based on the vehicle class rather than a taxi meter, and that price already includes tolls, fuel and the meet & greet service. There's nothing extra to pay when you arrive.

About Hotel Ambasador Split

Hotel Ambasador Split is a high-rise hotel directly on Bačvice beach, with sea-view rooms and immediate access to the sand, cafes and bars that make Bačvice one of Split's liveliest spots. Its location is hard to beat for combining a beach holiday with city sightseeing — Diocletian's Palace, the Riva waterfront and the ferry port are all within easy walking distance, so guests can move between beach and old town without needing a car.

Staying so close to central Split also makes it easy to explore further — see our other routes from Split Airport or add a private day trip to Plitvice Lakes or Krka National Park while you're here.
