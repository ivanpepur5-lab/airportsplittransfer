About Amphora Hotel Split

Amphora Hotel Split sits on Sustipan, a small pine-covered peninsula overlooking Split's harbour, giving it a quieter, greener setting than hotels directly on Bačvice beach while staying just a short walk from both the sand and the old town. Guests get resort-style comfort — pool, sea views and easy access to the waterfront promenade — combined with the convenience of being minutes from Diocletian's Palace and Split's ferry port. The peninsula's shaded walking paths also make it a pleasant base for morning runs or an evening stroll before dinner in the old town, without ever needing a car once you've arrived.

From Amphora Hotel Split it's also simple to see more of the coast — take a look at our other routes from Split Airport or book a private day trip to Plitvice Lakes or Krka National Park during your stay.
