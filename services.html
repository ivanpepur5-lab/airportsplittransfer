Services

Private Transfer Services

From a single airport pickup to multi-day itineraries, every service is built around a private vehicle, a fixed price and a professional driver.

Airport Arrivals

Your driver tracks your flight and waits at arrivals with a name board, ready to help with luggage from the terminal to the car.

Airport Departures

Scheduled pickup from your address timed to your flight, with a buffer built in for check-in and security.

Point-to-Point Transfers

Direct transfers between towns along the coast — Split to Makarska, Šibenik to Zadar, or any route in between.

Return Transfers

Book both legs of your trip together, with your return pickup pre-scheduled. Charged as two one-way transfers — no discount, no surprises.

Group & Wedding Transfers

The Renault Trafic and multiple V-Class vehicles can be combined for wedding parties, teams and larger groups travelling together.

Hourly & Full-Day Hire

A vehicle and driver at your disposal for touring, business itineraries or multi-stop day trips — contact us for a custom quote.
