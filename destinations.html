FAQ

Common Questions

How many destinations do you serve from Split Airport?

Dozens: from Split itself to Dubrovnik and Plitvice Lakes National Park, plus smaller coastal villages and marinas. Use the search box above to find yours.

Is the price the same in both directions?

Yes. Each destination card above links to both directions, "From Airport" and "To Airport", priced the same way, based on the exact pickup and drop-off address.

My destination isn't listed: can I still book a transfer?

Yes. We cover most of the Dalmatian coast beyond what's listed here, contact us with your address for a quote.

How is the exact price calculated?

The price is computed instantly from your precise pickup and drop-off address and chosen vehicle, enter the details in the booking form on the homepage for a fixed quote.

Can I search for my destination on this page?

Yes, type a town name into the search box above the destination cards to filter the list instantly.
