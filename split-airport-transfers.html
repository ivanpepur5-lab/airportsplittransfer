FAQ

Split Airport Transfers — Common Questions

How much does a private transfer from Split Airport cost?

The exact price depends on your pickup and drop-off address and the vehicle you choose. Enter your details in our booking form for an instant, fixed price — tolls, fuel and meet-and-greet at arrivals are always included, with no hidden fees.

How long does it take to get from Split Airport to Split city center?

The drive from Split Airport to Split city center takes about 20–25 minutes under normal traffic conditions.

Do you offer transfers to Trogir, Omiš, Makarska and other coastal towns?

Yes. We run private, fixed-price transfers from Split Airport to Trogir, Omiš, Makarska, Šibenik, Zadar, Novalja and every other major town along the Dalmatian coast.

Is the price really fixed, with no hidden fees?

Yes. The price is agreed before you travel, based on the vehicle class rather than a taximeter, and already includes tolls, fuel and meet-and-greet at arrivals.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight number in real time, so your driver adjusts the pickup automatically. There is no extra charge for reasonable delays.

Can I book a return transfer from my hotel to Split Airport?

Yes — select "Return" when booking to schedule both legs of your trip together. A return transfer automatically includes a 5% discount on the total price.

What vehicles are available for Split Airport transfers?

We offer a Sedan (Škoda Superb or similar, up to 4 passengers) and a Business Van (Mercedes-Benz V-Class, up to 7 passengers), both at a fixed price per vehicle.

How do I book a Split Airport transfer online?

Enter your pickup and drop-off address, choose your date, time and vehicle in the booking form below, and you'll see an instant fixed price — no account or deposit required to reserve.
