FAQ

Common Questions

How much does a private transfer from Split Airport cost?

The exact price depends on your pickup and drop-off address and the vehicle you choose. Enter your details in the booking form above for an instant, fixed price — tolls, fuel and meet-and-greet at arrivals are always included, with no hidden fees.

Which destinations do you cover from Split Airport?

We run private, fixed-price transfers from Split Airport to Split, Trogir, Omiš, Makarska, Šibenik, Zadar, Dubrovnik, Plitvice Lakes and every other major town and village along the Dalmatian coast.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight number in real time, so your driver adjusts the pickup automatically. There is no extra charge for reasonable delays.

Can I book a return transfer?

Yes — select "Return" when booking to schedule both legs of your trip together, with a 5% discount on the total price.

What vehicles can I choose from?

A Škoda Superb sedan (1–4 passengers), a Mercedes-Benz V-Class (1–7 passengers) or a Renault Trafic van (1–8 passengers), all at a fixed price per vehicle.

Are child seats available?

Yes, child seats are provided free of charge on request — just mention it when booking.

How do I pay for my transfer?

We accept both card and cash. The price is agreed and confirmed before you travel, so there are no surprises on the day.
