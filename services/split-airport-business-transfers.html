Made for work trips

Add your company's name, address and tax number in the notes when you book, and we issue the invoice in the company's name. Croatian companies get a standard R1 invoice. You can pay by card or in cash, and the price does not change on the day.

The Škoda Superb is a quiet executive sedan for up to 4 people. For a team or a client group, the Mercedes V-Class seats up to 7 in a premium cabin, with room for luggage and equipment.

We follow your flight, and the first 60 minutes after landing are free, so a delay or a slow passport line costs you nothing. For a day of meetings in Split, Trogir or Šibenik, a car and driver can stay with you by the hour or for the whole day.

Questions

Can I get an invoice for my company?

Yes. Give us the company name, address and tax number when you book, and we issue the invoice in the company's name. Croatian companies receive an R1 invoice.

Which car is best for a business trip?

The Škoda Superb for up to 4 people. For a team or clients, the Mercedes V-Class seats up to 7 in a premium cabin.

Can the driver wait during a meeting and take us back?

Yes. A car and driver can be booked by the hour or for the full day. Contact us with your plan for a quote.

What if my flight is delayed?

We follow your flight number and adjust the pickup. The first 60 minutes after landing are free.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, card and cash are both accepted, and the price is fixed when you book.
