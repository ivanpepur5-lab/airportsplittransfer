From the port to the airport

Split's port sits right next to the old town. Ferries and catamarans from the islands come in here, and cruise ships berth or anchor along the harbour. The airport is about 20 km away, usually 25 to 30 minutes by car, and longer on summer Saturdays when the island traffic peaks.

Ports are busy in summer, so we agree the exact meeting point with you before the day. Add your ship or ferry and the time you expect to be off in the notes when you book. If the ferry is running late, send us a message on WhatsApp and we adjust the pickup.

Going the other way? We also drive from Split Airport or your hotel to the port, timed to your ferry or cruise check-in. Book a transfer to the port.

How it works

Questions

How long is the drive from Split port to the airport?

About 25 to 30 minutes for roughly 20 km. Allow more time on summer Saturdays.

Where do we meet the driver at the port?

We agree the exact meeting point with you before the day, depending on where your ship or ferry comes in.

What if our ferry is late?

Tell us which ferry or catamaran you are on. If it is running late, message us on WhatsApp and we adjust the pickup.

Can you take us from the airport to the port?

Yes. Use the port as your destination when you book and add your ferry or check-in time, so we can plan a safe arrival.

Is the price per person?

No, per vehicle and fixed when you book, with tolls and fuel included.
