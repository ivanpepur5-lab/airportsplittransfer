Families with children

Split Airport transfers for families with kids

After a flight with small children, the last thing you need is a taxi queue or a bus change. Your driver meets you in arrivals, the child seats are already fitted, and you go straight to your door.

What we take care of

Child seats are free and fitted before you get in. When you book, add the number of children and their ages in the notes, so the driver brings the right seats. Croatian law requires a suitable seat for young children, and we always follow it.

Pushchairs, travel cots and your own car seats go in the boot with the suitcases. With a lot of luggage, choose the Mercedes V-Class or the Renault Trafic. The price is per vehicle, so the extra space does not mean paying per head.

Flights with children are often late, and passport control can be slow. We follow your flight, and the first 60 minutes after landing are free, so nobody has to rush.

How it works

  1. BookTap the button, enter your address and add the children's ages in the notes.
  2. We follow your flightIf you land early or late, your driver knows and adjusts.
  3. Met in arrivalsYour driver waits with a name board and helps with the bags and the pushchair.
  4. Straight to your doorNo other passengers. Child seats are ready in the car.

Planning a day out with the kids? See our private day trips to Krka and Plitvice, or compare the vehicles.

Questions

Are child seats really free?

Yes. Baby and child seats are free on request in all three vehicles. Tell us how many children are travelling and how old they are when you book.

Which vehicle is best for a family?

Up to 4 people with normal luggage fit in the Škoda Superb. With a pushchair, a travel cot or more than three suitcases, take the Mercedes V-Class (up to 7) or the Renault Trafic (up to 8).

What if our flight is late?

We follow your flight number, so the driver adjusts to the real landing time. The first 60 minutes after landing are free.

Do children pay extra?

No. The price is per vehicle and fixed when you book, with tolls, fuel and meet and greet included.

Can we book the ride back to the airport too?

Yes. Choose "Return" in the form to book both legs together and get 5% off the total.
